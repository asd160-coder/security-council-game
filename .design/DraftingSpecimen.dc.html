Choose the tone of your opening diplomatic line.

You are choosing a sentence, not a label. Read them.

Condemnatory and urgent The installation of offensive weapons in Cuba is an unacceptable act that demands immediate correction.
Measured but firm The developments in Cuba are of the gravest seriousness, and they require a response that is firm, deliberate, and proportionate.
Procedural and investigative The facts in Cuba must be established by means all parties can credit, and a process to verify them should begin without delay.
